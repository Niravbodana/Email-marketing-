📱 SMS Provider (Twilio) Not tested yet

⚠️ Honest note: India ke DND (National Do Not Disturb) registry ko koi free public API check nahi karta. Real Indian promotional SMS ke liye DLT-registered provider (jaise MSG91, Kaleyra, Gupshup) chahiye — wahi operator-level pe DND numbers automatically block karta hai. Twilio DLT-registered nahi hai India ke liye, isliye testing/non-Indian numbers ke liye theek hai.

🔗 Lead Webhook

Yeh URL + secret apne website developer ko do — jab bhi koi neercred.com/apply pe form submit kare, unka server yahan POST kare taaki hume asli lead pata chale (na ki sirf click). Body: { "secret": "...", "email": "...", "name": "..." }

Tip: Gmail/Outlook block bulk sending fast. For real bulk marketing, use a transactional ESP (SendGrid / Amazon SES / Mailgun / Brevo) SMTP credentials here for far better deliverability, and always set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC on your sending domain.

✉️ Templates

Multiple templates bana ke alag-alag bhejo — Dashboard/Templates list me har template ke sent count aur website click-through dikhega, jisse pata chalega konsa template zyada lead la raha hai.

🔘 Apply Button (optional)

Yeh ek bada, clickable button email ke end me add kar dega jo seedha diye gaye link (jaise neercred.com/apply) pe le jaata hai. Yeh link automatically track hoti hai — Templates list me dikh jayega kitno ne click kiya.

⚠️ Note: "Click here" aur "Apply now" jaisi wording spam filters ko trigger karti hai (upar wale Spam Words checker me bhi flag hoti hai). Kuch safer options: "Check Your Eligibility", "View Your Offer", "Start Your Application", "See Your Options" — link wahi neercred.com/apply rahega, sirf button text change hota hai.
